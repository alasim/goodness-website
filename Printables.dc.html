Printing tips

Colour printing

Enable "Print background colours and images" in your browser's print settings for full brand fidelity.

Paper size

Set paper to A4. The certificate uses landscape orientation — most browsers detect this automatically.

Scale & margins

Set scale to 100% and margins to "None" or "Minimum" for best results.
